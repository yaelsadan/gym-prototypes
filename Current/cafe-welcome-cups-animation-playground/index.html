Refinement sequence

  • approach → toast — cups start farther apart, rotate from a low anchor, kiss at the inner rims, then a 2–3px rebound and a short hold.
  • first-bubble → second-bubble → chat-hold — a small teardrop leaves the left rim, becomes a compact bubble, then the right follows ~200ms later. The pair holds as a stacked exchange.
  • bubbles-collapse → droplets-fall — each bubble’s tail retracts, the body returns to one drop, and the cups gather while those drops fall into the overlap.
  • final-settle — the two drops meet as the yellow overlap, then the lockup eases to the static Café mark.

What this pass does not change

Welcome copy, page layout, responsive behaviour, and the settled yellow / cream / olive palette stay as they are on the canonical screens. Integrate this motion into Desktop or Mobile only after this playground is reviewed.
