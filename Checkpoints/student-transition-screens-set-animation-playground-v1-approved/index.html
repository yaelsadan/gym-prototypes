Isolated study for the set transition screens. In 1st Set the ball drops between the open paddles, lands on the “s” of “1st”, rebounds, and is caught as the paddles close into the Gym mark. In 2nd Set it enters low on the left, bounces off the right edge and then higher off the left edge, and is lobbed into the closing paddles. In 3rd Set the ball is thrown in onto the left paddle, the paddles pass it twice while stepping in, and close around it. All three use the same rig as the Welcome animation. The ball, the paddles, and each title glyph are live elements on one timeline.

Mark is welcome-paddles.svg verbatim. Paddles open by 95 units and roll ±60.3°, the same stance as the Welcome rally. The yellow lens is the ball and morphs back into the lens on the catch. 1st Set: fall and rebound share one gravity, and contact is solved against the letter outlines, so the ball rides the live top edge of the squashing “s”. 2nd Set: one ballistic flight under a single gravity. The invisible edges are inset by the ball’s radius; each bounce damps only the horizontal speed, so after the soft left edge the ball floats over the left paddle and drops into the closing paddles. 3rd Set: thrown in from beyond the right edge, then two passes under the same gravity. The ball meets each paddle face as in the Welcome rally, and each hit is a short swing from the handle butt. Keys: 1 / 2 / 3 switch, R replays, Space pauses.
